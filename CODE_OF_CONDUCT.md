# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.
Be respectful, accept constructive technical feedback, and focus disagreement
on observable behavior and evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, and the publication of another person's private data are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate it and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to [info@rsitech.ai](mailto:info@rsitech.ai).
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. RSI Tech maintainers
are responsible for fair and proportionate enforcement.
